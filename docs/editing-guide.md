# Editing the WOY Consulting website

You edit the website at **https://woy-consulting.sanity.studio**. Sign in with the email or Google account you were invited with.

## The golden rule
Published entries can be read through Sanity’s public API. Draft text is restricted to authorised users, but photos and files can be downloaded by anyone who has the asset URL, even before you press Publish. Use Studio only for material approved for the public website. Keep confidential client material, contracts, internal decks, and private contact details in approved private storage.

## Changing a case study or a person
1. Open **Case studies** or **People** in the left sidebar and click the entry.
2. Edit the text. Required fields are marked; you cannot publish until they are filled.
3. Press **Publish** (bottom right). The website updates within a minute.
4. To change the order on the website, drag entries in the sidebar list.

Each case study also has a collapsed section, **Older case-study page (/case-studies)**. It controls the older page design. Leave its fields empty to reuse the main text automatically.

Each person has a similar collapsed section for the older practitioners page. Its highlight box only appears when all three highlight fields are filled.

## Changing Expertise or Our approach

1. Open **Expertise** or **Our approach** in the Studio sidebar. Opening a new page entry pre-fills the current website wording.
2. **Expertise** controls the introduction, four advisory areas, service lists, the label above example case studies, and the closing section.
3. **Our approach** controls the introduction, four stages, working principles, and the detailed logo philosophy.
4. Review the copy and press **Publish**. The site retains the existing layout, links and page addresses. The homepage sections have their own copy.

## Adding a document
- **For the Resources page:** open **Resources**, click **+**, fill in the title, category and date, upload the file (PDF, Word, PowerPoint or Excel), then Publish.
- **On a case study or profile:** scroll to **Downloadable document**, upload the file and, if you like, set the button label. Then Publish. A Download button appears on that page.

Until the first resource is published, the Resources page shows a short "new resources are on their way" message.

## Undoing a change
Open the entry, click the clock icon (**History**) at the top right, choose an earlier version and press **Restore**, then Publish.

## Photos
For portraits, click the crop icon and drag the circle onto the face. The website frames the photo around that point. Use PNG, JPEG or WebP images.

## Web addresses
Each case study and person has a **Web address** field. Leave it alone unless a page genuinely has to move: changing it changes the page's link, and old links stop working.

## Can't sign in?
If signing in ends with "Could not verify login request" or "Unable to verify authorization request state", your browser dropped a short-lived Sanity security cookie. Your account is fine.
1. Close every Sanity and Studio tab.
2. Open a private window (Chrome: Incognito; Edge: InPrivate), go to the Studio, and sign in in one go. The sign-in link is only valid for 15 minutes, and starting a second sign-in cancels the first.
3. If that works, your usual browser is blocking it. Allow cookies for `[*.]sanity.io` and switch off ad or privacy blockers for sanity.io.

Never use "Sign up" to get around this: it creates a second, empty account with no access.

## Something looks wrong?
Restore the previous version (see above), then contact the website developer with the page address and a screenshot.
